How the estimate is made

An R(2+1)D-18 video network, fine-tuned on EchoNet-Dynamic, scores up to three 32-frame windows (every second frame, about one heartbeat each) and reports their mean. The spread shows how much the windows disagree.

Research prototype, not a medical device. Do not use for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Source on GitHub
