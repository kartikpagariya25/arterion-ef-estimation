About the model

R(2+1)D-18 (Kinetics-pretrained) fine-tuned on EchoNet-Dynamic. For each video it scores up to three 32-frame windows (every 2nd frame, about one heartbeat each) and reports their mean; the spread shows how much the windows disagree. The video is resized to 112×112.

Research prototype — not a medical device. Do not use for diagnosis or treatment decisions. · Source on GitHub
